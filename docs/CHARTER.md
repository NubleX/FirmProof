# Secure IoT Ledger – Governance Charter (Draft)

## Mission
Provide a neutral, public-good ledger of IoT device identities, firmware provenance, and security posture, to reduce global risk and reward responsible behavior.

## Principles
- **Neutrality:** Vendor-agnostic, region-agnostic, evidence-first.
- **Transparency:** Public scores, public budgets, auditable processes.
- **Open Standards:** W3C DID/VC, SPDX/CycloneDX, open APIs.
- **Safety:** Coordinated disclosure by default; no zero-day weaponization.

## On-chain Governance
- **Houses:** Manufacturers, Researchers, Public. High-impact proposals require cross-house quorum.
- **Referenda:** OpenGov-style proposals, on-chain discussion period, approval & enactment delays.
- **Emergency Response:** Security Response Team (SRT) can enact temporary fixes (7–30 days) subject to retroactive referendum.
- **Treasury:** Funds bounties, indexers, audits, public goods; transparent disbursements.

## Off-chain Structure
- **Foundation (Swiss Verein)** custodies trademarks, domains, fiat; publishes annual audits.
- **Conflict of Interest:** Mandatory disclosures for maintainers and council delegates.

## Upgrades & Standards
- Runtime upgradable via governance (Wasm). Breaking changes require RFC/ADR and dual-majority across Houses.

## Revocation & Censure
- Bad-faith actors (plagiarism, extortion) can have credentials/badges revoked via due process.
